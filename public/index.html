Is this you?

Women who know their work inside out and want to build for it.

You've been working for a while. You run a team, a process or a business, and you can see exactly where the time goes. You don't need a technical background. You need a real problem and the time to build.

  • 03You can use what you build at work, and your organization is behind you.

Questions

The things people ask before they apply.

Anything else, ask on the call if you're shortlisted, or email creativej.me@gmail.com.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You'll build with AI tools that work in plain language. What matters is knowing your work and being willing to try things.

How does applying work?

First, a two-minute application: who you are, your role and team, and who would cover the cost. If you're selected for the next step, we email you a few questions about the problem you'd bring. From those answers, Jitakshi invites a small group to a 20-minute call. Offers go out December 18.

What counts as a "real problem"?

Something that happens regularly in your work, costs time or money, and is small enough to have a working first version by week five. A weekly report, a repeated request, a process with too many steps. If you're not sure, apply anyway and tell us the area.

How much time is it, really?

About nine hours a week: two sessions of 2.5 hours at Volta, and about four hours of building in between. The one-on-one is 30 minutes, usually inside a working day.

Who pays, and when?

A full seat is $2,200 plus HST. Volta sponsors half of every seat, so you pay $1,100 plus HST. We strongly recommend your organization covers it, and if you move to the next step we ask who approves it, so we can confirm with them. If you're paying yourself, that's fine too. Payment is due by January 31, once your seat is confirmed. If you withdraw before February 9, your seat goes to someone on the waitlist and you're refunded. Once the cohort has started, the fee stands.

Can I use my company's data?

Only if your organization allows it. If not, you build with sample or anonymized data and switch to real data afterwards, with approval.

Which AI tools will we use?

Mainly Claude. Each participant needs a Claude Pro subscription for the two months, which is reimbursed when you complete the program. If your project needs more, we'll talk about it.

What if I miss a session?

Each week builds on the last, so we ask you to attend every session. If something unavoidable comes up, tell Jitakshi ahead of time and catch up with the materials before the next session.

Who owns the tool I build?

Your organization, since it's built for your work. You keep the skills.

Is it only for women?

Yes. Cohort one is ten women in mid-career or senior roles, learning alongside people at a similar stage.
